AcousticSpace: Deepfake Audio Detection via Room Impulse Response (RIR)

Addressing the fundamental vulnerability of vocal-only deepfake detectors by leveraging forensic residual physics and environmental acoustic consistency. While generative neural vocoders can mimic human formant frequencies, pitch contour, and vocal tract resonance, they consistently struggle to model the physically coupled, multi-path acoustic reflections that occur within real physical enclosures.

Python 3.13 Librosa 0.10 PyTorch HuggingFace AST FastAPI ASVspoof 2019/2021 Schroeder Integration

Mathematical Physics

Core Acoustic Formulas & Forensic Formulations

1. Acoustic Convolutive Model

y(t) = s(t) * h(t) + n(t)

Recorded audio $y(t)$ is the mathematical convolution ($\ast$) of the clean vocal source $s(t)$ and the enclosure's Room Impulse Response $h(t)$, plus additive background noise $n(t)$. Deepfakes synthesize $s(t)$ directly, decoupling it from any physical $h(t)$.

2. Schroeder Backward Integration (RT60)

E(t) = ∫_t^∞ h²(τ) dτ

Calculates the decay of sound energy without noise fluctuations. The time taken for $10 \log_{10}(E(t)/E(0))$ to drop from -5 dB to -35 dB is multiplied by 2 to determine the true reverberation time $RT_{60}$.

3. Direct-to-Reverberant Ratio (DRR)

DRR = 10 · log&sub1;&sub0; [ ∫&sub0;^{t_d} h²(t) dt / ∫_{t_d}^∞ h²(t) dt ]

Quantifies the ratio between the direct path energy (arrival within boundary $t_d \approx 2.5\text{ms}$) and the reflected reverberant field. Artificial AI voices consistently exhibit anomalous DRR values > +30 dB.

4. Sabine's Reverberation Formula

RT&sub6;&sub0; = 0.161 · V / ( ∑ S_i · α_i )

Relates room volume $V$ (m³) and total surface boundary absorption ($\sum S_i \alpha_i$) to expected reverberation time. Discrepancies between environmental context and audio acoustics expose manipulated speech.

Examination Guide

B.Tech Viva Voce Defense – Frequently Asked Questions

Essential technical answers for external examiner reviews
Q1: Why is checking Room Impulse Response (RIR) superior to checking vocal pitch or formant anomalies? +
Modern deep learning generative models (such as VALL-E, Bark, ElevenLabs, and CosyVoice) are trained extensively on human voice timbre, pitch contours, and phoneme transitions. They can easily deceive traditional vocal-only classifiers. However, these models generate audio in an "acoustic vacuum" or with superficial reverberation algorithms that violate fundamental wave equation physics (Schroeder energy decay, boundary absorption, and quadratic reflection density). RIR detection examines the physical enclosure rather than the vocal cords.
Q2: How do you extract Room Impulse Response from a single microphone speech recording without playing an impulse test? +
We use Blind RIR Estimation via homomorphic cepstral deconvolution and maximum likelihood estimation (MLE). In the complex cepstrum domain, convolution turns into addition: $\hat{y}(n) = \hat{s}(n) + \hat{h}(n)$. Because speech $s(n)$ has localized pitch harmonics while the room impulse response $h(n)$ forms a diffuse exponential decay, we use liftering filters and linear prediction residuals to isolate the room decay parameters without needing test chirps.
Q3: What datasets are used to train and validate AcousticSpace? +
We evaluate using the ASVspoof 2019 / 2021 Logical Access (LA) and WaveFake benchmarks, supplemented with controlled room impulse responses from the ACE (Acoustic Characterization, Exploitation) Corpus and OpenSLR RIR dataset to provide calibrated ground-truth room reverberation parameters across known room volumes and absorption profiles.
Q4: Why does the project use Audio Spectrogram Transformer (AST) instead of a simple 2D CNN (ResNet)? +
Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have limited receptive fields defined by local kernel sizes ($3\times3$ or $5\times5$), making them less effective at capturing long-range temporal reverberation tails that smear across hundreds of milliseconds. AST utilizes global multi-head self-attention across non-overlapping $16\times16$ spectrogram patches, allowing the network to correlate early direct sound packets with distant late diffuse reflections across the entire time-frequency plane.
Q5: Does a room acoustic mismatch guarantee that an audio file is an AI deepfake? +
No, and this is an important technical distinction for our project defense: acoustic anomalies can also stem from legitimate post-processing, aggressive noise gating, multi-microphone cross-fades, or poor compression codecs. Therefore, AcousticSpace reports a forensic confidence score and flags acoustic discontinuities as forensic evidence for investigative analysts, rather than making an unsubstantiated absolute legal claim.
